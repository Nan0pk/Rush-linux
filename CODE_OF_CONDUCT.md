# Code of Conduct

Rush Linux adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, as its code of conduct.

The full text is available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project maintainers. See `SECURITY.md` for the current contact
of record. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

> Note: a dedicated enforcement contact and response process is part of the
> project-sustainability plan (`docs/project-sustainability.md`, item C1) and
> will be filled in as governance is formalised.
